The International Media and Information Literacy Institute (IMILI), a pioneering UNESCO Category 2 Institute on Media and Information Literacy (MIL), under its flagship **Africa Against Xenophobia Project (AfAX-P)**, is pleased to invite interested persons to participate in its **"Digital Storytelling for Peace Building."** This initiative invites young Africans to harness the power of digital media to create compelling narratives that promote unity, tolerance, and peace; actively countering xenophobia, mis/disinformation and hate across the continent.

AfAX-P is IMILI's strategic response to address misinformation-driven xenophobia and hates that threaten Africa's social cohesion, regional integration, and economic stability. Recognizing the immense potential of youth and digital platforms, participants are encouraged to create original digital stories that explore and promote themes of unity, tolerance, peaceful coexistence, and the rejection of xenophobia and mis/disinformation within the African context. Stories should highlight the importance of responsible digital communication across African countries, that celebrates Africa's diversity, and inspire collective action towards a more harmonious future.

## Who Can Participate?

- **Age:** Applicants must be between 15 and 25 years old at the time of submission.
- **Nationality:** Open to all Africans within and out the Continent.
- **Originality:** Submissions must be original works of the participant(s) and not previously published or awarded.
- **Language:** Submissions can be in English, French, Arabic, Portuguese, or any African language, provided English subtitles are included for non-English entries.

## Submission Categories

Participants can submit their digital stories in one of the following categories:

| Category | Description | Duration/Format |
| -------- | ----------- | --------------- |
| Video | Short films, documentaries, animations/videos, or vlogs. | MP4 format, minimum 720p resolution. Maximum 3 minutes |
| Podcast/Audio Drama | Original audio narratives, interviews, or soundscapes. | MP3 or WAV format, clear audio quality. Maximum 3 minutes |
| Interactive Media | Web-based stories, digital comics, interactive presentations, or games. | Provide a functional URL and any necessary access instructions. Accessible via web link |
| Photography | Original photographs. | Must be high-resolution (JPEG or PNG). Minor digital enhancements are allowed, but AI-generated images must be declared. |

## How to Participate

- **Prepare Your Entry:** Create an original piece of work in one of the four categories that addresses the theme of combating Xenophobia and building peace.
- **Submit Online:** Access our secure online submission portal (……..).
- **Share on social media:** Post your entry on your preferred social media platforms, tagging IMILI and using official hashtags (#AfricaAgainstXenophobia #Imilinstitute).

## Assessment Criteria

Submissions will be evaluated based on:

- **Relevance to Theme (20%):** How well does your entry address unity, tolerance and peace?
- **Creativity and Originality (25%):** Does your work offer a unique perspective or innovative approach?
- **Impact and Message (25%):** Is your message clear, compelling, and resonant with a global youth audience?
- **Technical Quality (15%):** How well is your entry executed within its chosen medium?
- **Social Media Engagement (15%):** The number of likes, shares, and comments your entry receives when posted on social media platforms.

## Prizes and Recognition

- **Top 20 Winners:** Cash prizes, mentorship opportunities with leading media professionals, and a feature in IMILI's continental publications.
- **All Finalists:** Certificate of recognition to all participants, and promotion of the top 20 winners across AfAX-P activities, and invitation to an exclusive virtual workshop where the winners tell the stories behind their submissions.
- **Publication:** Selected stories will be showcased on the IMILI website and potentially integrated into IMILI's educational resources.
- **Dissemination Support:** Finalists would be supported to use their submissions to engage with their chose for Africa Against Xenophobia campaign
