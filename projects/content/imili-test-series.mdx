The **IMILI Test Series** provides structured assessments and evaluation tools for media and information literacy. The programme supports educators, trainers, and institutions in measuring literacy outcomes and tracking progress over time.

Further details on test formats, registration, and partnership opportunities will be published here as the programme rolls out across IMILI partner networks.
